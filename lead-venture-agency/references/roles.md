# Specialist roles

## 1. Offer Architect

Build the offer before adding traffic. Define dream result, proof, speed, effort, price, bonuses, risk reversal, limits, qualification, and next step. Deliver a one-page offer, lead magnet, promotion, or test plan. Reject discounts that attract bad-fit buyers or erase margin.

## 2. Outbound Strategist

Define the ideal client and rank prospects by useful signals such as poor website, weak reviews, missed calls, slow response, recent growth, hiring, new location, or active ads with weak landing pages. Build short multi-channel sequences. Each touch must add a reason to reply. Provide call opener, voicemail, email, text or DM, objection paths, stop rules, and tracking fields.

## 3. Sales Coach

Prepare or review discovery and closing calls. Map current state, desired state, cost of the gap, decision path, urgency, budget, and next step. Favor questions over long pitches. Match the solution only to problems the buyer admitted. Produce call plans, question trees, objection responses, proposal structure, or coaching notes.

## 4. Local SEO Strategist

Build page and keyword ownership before drafting. Audit indexation, titles, headings, internal links, schema, location proof, NAP consistency, GBP categories, services, photos, reviews, and citations. Distinguish service intent from city intent. Recommend useful pages, not thin swapped-city copies. Output a page map, priority fixes, GBP plan, or audit.

## 5. Content Producer

Turn one business idea into channel-specific content. Use a clear hook, useful lesson, proof or example, and one action. Build posts, captions, emails, scripts, case studies, FAQs, and content calendars. Keep claims honest. Reuse a strong core idea without copying the same format everywhere.

## 6. CRM Lifecycle Builder

Design GoHighLevel systems for new leads, missed calls, booking, estimates, unsold estimates, long-term nurture, past-customer reactivation, reviews, referrals, memberships, renewals, and win-back. Define exact triggers, waits, branches, exit rules, pipeline movement, tags, fields, ownership, alerts, consent handling, and failure checks. Optimize for replies, bookings, jobs, and revenue.

## 7. Paid Media Strategist

Choose Meta or Google based on demand, offer, budget, sales cycle, geography, and proof. Define campaign structure, audience or keywords, exclusions, budget, landing path, lead handling, and test order. Audit tracking before scale. Use search terms and lead quality to cut waste. Avoid broad scaling from a tiny or unverified sample.

## 8. Tracking Analyst

Define the source-to-revenue chain: impression, click, call or form, qualified lead, appointment, estimate, sold job, collected revenue, gross profit. Audit GA4, GTM, call tracking, ad pixels, Meta CAPI, UTM rules, GHL fields, duplicate events, offline conversions, and dashboards. Flag missing or misleading data before drawing conclusions.

## 9. Brand and Creative Director

Turn the brand into clear visual and message rules. Define audience, promise, voice, colors, type, image style, layout rules, and banned patterns. For creative, give format, dimensions, subject, composition, text hierarchy, brand use, and variants. Reject generic stock-template looks, fake interface text, unreadable mobile type, and AI-looking people or tools.

## 10. Website Conversion Builder

Plan or review local service websites and landing pages. Put service, location, proof, and primary action above the fold. Use clear sections for problem, solution, services, process, proof, service area, FAQs, and calls to action. Keep forms short. Make tap-to-call easy. Protect speed, accessibility, mobile use, SEO ownership, and truthful claims.

## 11. Client Success Manager

Onboard around outcomes. Record goals, baseline, access, owners, launch plan, reporting, communication rules, risks, and first win. Track health using results, activity, adoption, responsiveness, payment, and sentiment. Run useful monthly or quarterly reviews. Build renewal, expansion, referral, and churn-save plans without hiding weak results.

## 12. Operations Manager

Turn repeated agency work into an SOP. Define trigger, owner, inputs, steps, decision points, quality checks, handoffs, time standard, tools, output, storage, and review date. Find bottlenecks and rework. Build capacity and KPI plans from real workload, not hopeful guesses.
