# Agency scorecards

## Funnel math

Use the narrowest reliable denominator.

- Contact rate = contacted leads / total leads
- Booking rate = booked appointments / contacted leads
- Show rate = completed appointments / booked appointments
- Close rate = sold jobs / completed estimates or qualified opportunities
- Cost per lead = ad spend / leads
- Cost per qualified lead = ad spend / qualified leads
- Cost per booked appointment = ad spend / booked appointments
- Customer acquisition cost = total acquisition cost / new customers
- Return on ad spend = attributed revenue / ad spend
- Gross-profit return = attributed gross profit / ad spend

State when attribution is incomplete. Do not mix booked revenue with collected revenue.

## Audit priority

Score each issue:

- Impact: 1 to 5
- Confidence: 1 to 5
- Ease: 1 to 5

Priority score = impact multiplied by confidence multiplied by ease.

Also mark blockers that must be fixed first, such as broken forms, bad phone routing, missing tracking, no lead owner, wrong service area, or ads pointing to an unrelated page.

## Client health

Rate each area red, yellow, or green:

- Business results
- Lead quality
- Lead response
- Sales follow-through
- Deliverable progress
- Client responsiveness
- Platform adoption
- Payment status
- Client sentiment
- Renewal risk

Never hide a red result inside an average score. Give the cause, owner, next action, and review date.

## Website quality gate

- Main service, city, proof, and action are clear in five seconds
- Phone and form work on mobile
- Page has one clear search intent
- Titles, headings, links, schema, and index settings are correct
- Copy is specific to the trade and location
- Claims and reviews are real
- Images look real and fit the brand
- No fake names, fake interface data, broken text, or generic filler
- Load speed and layout are acceptable on mobile
- Tracking fires once and records the correct source
