# Agency playbooks

## New client 90-day plan

1. Confirm goals, service mix, area, capacity, close rate, job value, margins, seasonality, budget, and past data.
2. Confirm whether the stated budget is media spend only or must also cover agency fees, software, website work, creative, and tracking. Do not silently split it across these costs.
3. Fix tracking and lead handling leaks.
4. Tighten the offer and landing path.
5. Capture easy demand through GBP, website fixes, reactivation, reviews, and referrals.
6. Launch one paid or outbound channel with a clear test budget.
7. Review qualified leads, booked jobs, sold work, revenue, and gross profit weekly.
8. Scale only after the full path works.

## TradeSite build

1. Brand and buyer brief
2. Service and city page ownership map
3. Site structure and conversion path
4. Copy and proof collection
5. Design direction and image plan
6. Build and mobile review
7. Forms, calls, tracking, schema, and index settings
8. Quality gate before launch
9. GBP, citations, reviews, and content plan after launch

## GoHighLevel workflow spec

Provide a table with:

| Field | Required detail |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | One business result |
| Trigger | Exact event entering the workflow |
| Filters | Who is included and excluded |
| Steps | Exact messages and internal actions |
| Timing | Delay before each step |
| Branches | Reply, booking, no response, bad number, opt-out |
| Records | Tags, fields, notes, attribution, opportunity stage |
| Owner | Person responsible for alerts and replies |
| Exit | Conditions that stop all messages |
| Success | Event counted as the win |
| QA | Test contact, devices, links, sender, time zone, duplicates |

## Paid campaign launch

1. Confirm economics and capacity.
2. Verify conversion tracking and call handling.
3. Pick one offer and one audience or intent group.
4. Build a matching landing page or lead form.
5. Launch a small set of meaningfully different ads.
6. Tag sources in GHL and record qualified lead outcomes.
7. Cut clear waste, fix the funnel, then raise budget in controlled steps.

## Monthly client report

Lead with business results. Include:

1. Calls and forms
2. Qualified leads
3. Appointments or estimates
4. Sold jobs
5. Collected or attributed revenue when reliable
6. Spend and cost per qualified lead or sold job
7. Ranking, traffic, GBP, and content changes as supporting facts
8. What worked
9. What did not work
10. Three actions for the next month, each with an owner

## Past-customer reactivation

1. Clean and segment the list by service, last job, location, value, consent, and active customer status.
2. Choose a timely reason to contact them.
3. Make one simple offer or question.
4. Use text and email only where allowed.
5. Stop on reply, booking, sale, opt-out, bad data, or current open opportunity.
6. Route replies to a named person fast.
7. Track replies, qualified opportunities, bookings, sales, revenue, opt-outs, and complaints.
