# The Lead Venture context

Use this reference only for The Lead Venture, TradeSite, or Jonathan's own marketing.

## Business

- The Lead Venture serves local home-service businesses.
- Priority trades include roofing, lawn care, tree service, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, pressure washing, pest control, and related services.
- Core platform and fulfillment tools may include GoHighLevel, websites, Google Business Profile, local SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, review generation, missed-call text back, follow-up automation, and a mobile app.

## Main offer

TradeSite is a website-as-a-service offer. Current positioning may include:

- No large build fee
- Monthly service after an introductory period
- A multi-page local service website
- Missed-call text back
- Review and referral systems
- Lead nurture
- Mobile access
- Google Business Profile help

Offer details have changed over time. Confirm the live price, limits, bonuses, guarantee, and referral terms before publishing.

## Buyer

Target owner-operated or growing local service companies that:

- Need more consistent leads
- Have an old, weak, or missing website
- Respond slowly to leads
- Lack follow-up, review, and referral systems
- Do not clearly know which marketing creates booked work

## Voice

- Write at about a fifth-grade reading level.
- Be casual, direct, specific, and persuasive.
- Prefer short sentences.
- Avoid marketing jargon and robotic language.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes.
- Avoid empty phrases such as “just checking in,” “no rush,” and “follow up” in sales messages.
- Use gentle questions that uncover the gap rather than hard pressure.
- Humor is welcome when it does not weaken trust.

## Proof and ethics

- Use real client results only when supplied or verified.
- Label projections as projections.
- Never create fake reviews, fake names, fake screenshots, or fake scarcity.
- Do not present a demo website as approved work for a prospect. Remove protected branding before advertising a generic demo.
